## PITFALLS
- Hard-coding DOH's 40/50/10 weighting, 130%-tempo, or calendar dates: these live in the AGENCY's CSC-approved SPMS manual, not in CSC MC 6; another tenant (or a DOH revision) will have different values â€” they must be tenant configuration with an effective-date history.
- Treating SPCR as a universal CSC form: it exists only inside agency systems (in DOH, formally for Level III hospital sections). Building a mandatory four-tier cascade would be wrong for most offices; the tier count must be configurable per office type.
- Letting individual final ratings be issued before the final office assessment exists, or allowing the average of individual ratings to exceed the office rating â€” HR is required to reject the Summary List in that case.
- Averaging Q/E/T when a dimension is not applicable to an indicator (e.g., timeliness-only indicators): A must average only the applicable dimensions, so a NULL-aware average is required, and dimension applicability is set at target-setting time.
- Allowing ratings on line items without attached MOVs â€” under both CSC MC 6 and DOH DO 2019-0440 an unproven task is disregarded entirely, not given a low score.
- Forgetting the all-or-nothing exception (targets that cannot be exceeded score 5 if met, 2 if missed) and the mandatory written justification for any 5 (Outstanding) or 2 (Unsatisfactory) dimension/final rating in DOH.
- Modeling only the year-end flow: commitments (OPCR/DPCR/IPCR targets) must be signed and approved BEFORE the period starts; a system that lets targets be created retroactively invites the exact audit finding SPMS was designed to prevent.
- Missing the notice clocks for adverse action: US notice within 30 days after semester end, Poor preliminary rating within 15 days after the 3rd month â€” miss these and the agency cannot lawfully drop the employee, and the Head of Office is personally liable for neglect of duty.
- Locking the office rating too early or too late: it is contestable only at the annual performance review conference and immutable afterward; individual ratings follow the separate 10-day-appeal / 30-day-PMT-decision clocks.
- Assuming employees self-rate: CSC MC 6 explicitly removes self-rating â€” employees record actual accomplishments, supervisors rate; UI roles that let ratees enter their own Q/E/T scores misrepresent the workflow.
- Reusing 'rating period' semantics across forms: in DOH the OPCR is annual (with quarterly monitoring) while DPCR/SPCR/IPCR are semestral â€” a single uniform period model breaks the OPCR flow.
- Ignoring edge-case raters: staff on detail/secondment are rated by the actual office (consolidated where they spent the majority of the period); staff leaving on travel/scholarship with â‰¥90 days must file before departure; sub-90-day periods are not ratable.
