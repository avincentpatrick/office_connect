## PITFALLS
- Hardcoding BED deadlines: the exact November dates (e.g., Nov 15/Nov 29 in CL 2016-9) are reissued every year in a new DBM Circular Letter â€” store per-fiscal-year deadline overrides, never constants.
- Treating Office-Connect as the submission channel: the legal system of record for BEDs and BFARs is the DBM Unified Reporting System (URS); the module is a preparation/consolidation layer and must export URS-encodable figures, not claim compliance by itself.
- Confusing obligation with disbursement: FAR 1 tracks appropriations â†’ allotments â†’ OBLIGATIONS â†’ DISBURSEMENTS â†’ balances as distinct columns; a WFP schema with a single 'amount spent' field cannot produce any FAR and will fail COA review.
- A DOH bureau (like BLHSD) is an operating unit inside the DOH central office, not a direct DBM-reporting agency â€” its WFP and accomplishment data roll UP to DOH-OSEC/HPDPB (via the DOH online WFP system and DM-prescribed templates), which consolidates for DBM; the module must support this two-tier consolidation rather than assume direct DBM submission.
- Missing the trust-fund fork: FAR 4 is due the 30th day after month-end normally, but the 10th day for implementing agencies reporting on inter-agency transferred funds (JC 2019-1); FAR 2/2-A apply only when off-budget funds exist and FAR 6 only when trust receipts exist â€” these reports must be feature-flagged per fund cluster, defaulting OFF.
- FAR 3 (Aging of Due and Demandable Obligations) is ANNUAL (30 days after year-end), unlike the quarterly FAR 1 family â€” do not put it on the quarterly cycle.
- PREXC/UACS codes change across fiscal years (program restructuring, new organization codes); P/A/P reference data must be versioned per fiscal year, not global.
- BED 2 targets and BAR 1 actuals must use the SAME indicator rows â€” if users can edit indicators mid-year without versioning, quarterly variance computation breaks and COA/DBM will bounce the report.
- Assuming 'MRR' means Monitoring and Review Report: in an ISO-certified DOH bureau the established artifact is the ISO 9001:2015 Clause 9.3 Management Review Report; confirm with the tenant but default to the ISO construct with its mandated input/output lists.
- WFP totals that don't tie: every quarterly financial cut must sum to the annual allotment per P/A/P per allotment class, and monthly disbursement program (BED 3) must not exceed the obligation program (BED 1) cumulatively â€” these are arithmetic validations DBM reviewers apply manually today.
- Scanned-PDF form replication: official DBM annex layouts (Annex A = BAR 1, etc.) are Excel matrices with merged headers; generating them as free-form PDFs makes URS encoding harder â€” generate Excel matching the annex column order first, PDF second.
