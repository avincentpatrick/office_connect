## PITFALLS
- Building the module around RA 9184 artifacts â€” old PPMP/APP formats, 'Shopping', SVP-as-negotiated-procurement, P1M SVP ceilings. The NGPA standard forms are mandatory since 21 September 2025; only procurement activities commenced before NGPA effectivity stay under RA 9184 (Sec. 113 transitory), so a per-record regime flag is needed, not a global assumption.
- Treating the APP as one mutable document. The regime requires distinct Indicative, Final, and Updated (versioned, dated) APPs, and each Updated APP must contain ALL items (changed + unchanged) with changed rows highlighted and changed values bolded â€” so the schema needs immutable versions plus a computed diff, which also aligns with the project's append-only audit-chain rules.
- Hardcoding thresholds (P2M SVP, P200k Direct Acquisition) or the negotiated-procurement instance list â€” RA 12009 explicitly makes these GPPB-adjustable; store them as effectivity-dated configuration.
- Putting itemized CSE lines in the main APP. CSE bought from PS-DBM appears in the main APP only as summary totals; the detail goes in the separate APP-CSE Form (plus 'Other Items' form) uploaded to mPhilGEPS on a much earlier deadline (~31 Aug of the prior year) â€” missing this split is the most common real-world agency error.
- Modeling schedule columns as exact dates. The official forms use MM/YYYY month precision for start/end of procurement activity and delivery/implementation; storing day-precision dates and formatting them as full dates would deviate from the prescribed forms.
- Forgetting the Market Scoping Checklist gate â€” it is a mandatory PPMP attachment under the new forms (IRR Secs. 7.7.1(g) and 10); allowing PPMP submission without it produces non-compliant plans.
- Letting EPA-flagged lines proceed to award before funding effectivity â€” IRR Sec. 12 forbids award until the GAA/budget is approved and the Final APP is HoPE-approved.
- Assuming the app submits directly to regulators. GPPB accepts APP/PMR only via its Microsoft Forms portal (cloud-storage share links), and PS-DBM only via mPhilGEPS upload â€” the app's job is generating compliant .xls/.xlsx + signed .pdf files and tracking the human submission, not API integration (none exists).
- Dropping by-administration projects from the PPMP â€” they must still be listed (mode = N/A, undertaken by administration) because the PPMP documents whether each PAP is implemented through procurement or in-house.
- Ignoring lot-level granularity â€” each lot of a multi-lot project occupies its own row in both PPMP and APP, with per-lot budgets; a single-row-per-project schema cannot reproduce the forms.
- Missing the 'no procurement outside the APP' rule (IRR Sec. 7.8) â€” if other Office-Connect modules (Supply Management, future PR/PO flows) can create purchase requests that do not reference an approved APP line, the platform itself enables audit findings.
