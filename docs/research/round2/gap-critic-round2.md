# agent9
## MISSING AREAS (gap critic)
- GAD Plan & Budget and GAD Accomplishment Report (Magna Carta of Women): The digest only mentions GAD as a UACS activity tag and cites the superseded PCW-NEDA-DBM JC 2012-01; the operative issuance is PCW-DBM-NEDA JMC 2022-01, which requires an annual GAD Plan & Budget (>=5% of total appropriations, encoded/submitted via the PCW Gender Mainstreaming Monitoring System) with HGDG attribution scoring of programs, and a GAD Accomplishment Report submitted every January. The WFP module needs GPB/GAD-AR forms, HGDG score capture per attributed activity, and the GMMS submission calendar.
- Transparency Seal full posting checklist (GAA General Provisions): Only quarterly BAR/FAR posting is covered; the GAA GP Transparency Seal requires posting of agency mandate/officials, approved budgets and targets, budget modifications, APP and contracts awarded with winning suppliers, major programs/projects with target beneficiaries, status-of-implementation/assessment reports, FOI Manual and FOI reports, annual reports on retained income, System of Ranking Delivery Units (PBB), and SALN review-and-compliance procedure. A postings/compliance checklist with per-item cadence should be a first-class output of the unified reports module.
- Citizen's Charter creation, maintenance and Certificate of Compliance (RA 11032/ARTA): The CSM research presumes a Citizen's Charter exists but nothing covers maintaining one: services with checklists of requirements, processing times, fees and responsible persons in the prescribed handbook/billboard/online formats, mandatory updating whenever services change, the annually audited Certificate of Compliance signed by the agency head submitted to ARTA, and designation of a Committee on Anti-Red Tape (CART). The service catalog planned for CSM should double as the CC source of truth and generate the CC handbook.
- PhilGEPS posting and registration obligations under RA 12009: Coverage stops at the APP-CSE upload to mPhilGEPS; missing are the PhilGEPS obligations attached to actual procurement activity that the Supply/PPMP modules will trigger: agency registration, posting of procurement opportunities, notices of award, and contract/award information in PhilGEPS, and capturing PhilGEPS reference numbers as evidence on PRs/POs (a standard COA audit check even if the platform never integrates via API).
- APCPI annual procurement self-assessment: The GPPB's Agency Procurement Compliance and Performance Indicator (APCPI) annual self-assessment is absent; FY2025 submission is suspended pending an NGPA-updated tool (GPPB Public Advisory 04-2026), but the module should still be planned to accumulate the indicator inputs APCPI scores (procurement lead times, failed-bidding rates, APP-vs-actual variance, PMR timeliness) so the assessment is a report, not a manual reconstruction.
- Budget preparation cycle: National Budget Call, BP Forms and OSBPS: The digest covers budget execution (BED/BAR/FAR) but not budget preparation, which is when Indicative PPMPs and proposed WFPs are actually crafted: the annual DBM National Budget Call, Tier 1/Tier 2 two-tier budgeting, the BP Form series agencies fill out, and submission via DBM's Online Submission of Budget Proposal System (OSBPS). The WFP module's fiscal-year lifecycle should start at budget call, not at GAA enactment.
- COA year-end financial statements and supporting schedules the supply module must feed: Not covered: NGAs submit year-end financial statements and trial balances to COA by February 14 (per fund cluster, per GAM Vol I and COA circulars) plus monthly/quarterly trial balances, and the supply module's registers must produce the supporting schedules auditors reconcile â€” PPE schedule vs RPCPPE vs PPELC, inventory and semi-expendable schedules vs RPCI/RPCSP vs ledger cards. The module should export these reconciliation schedules at year-end.
- COA audit findings lifecycle: AOMs, AAPSI, and Notices of Suspension/Disallowance/Charge: No coverage of tracking COA Audit Observation Memoranda and management responses, the Agency Action Plan and Status of Implementation (AAPSI) on Annual Audit Report recommendations (due within 60 days of AAR receipt, with periodic status reporting), or NS/ND/NC records with their 6-month appeal windows under the COA Rules. These are natural feeds into the Risk Registry and Management Review and a core compliance-calendar item.
- PBB operational mechanics beyond eligibility (AO 25 IATF annual MC): The digest mentions PBB eligibility in passing but not the annual AO 25 MC machinery the performance module could serve: the System of Ranking Delivery Units, per-year agency accountability requirements checklist, and the forced-ranking/isolation of delivery-unit performance that consumes OPCR/DPCR data. At minimum, plan a PBB-ranking export and a per-year configurable accountability checklist.
- DOH program reporting systems BLHSD itself operates (LGU Health Scorecard, LHS Maturity Levels): BLHSD manages the LGU Health Scorecard and the Local Health Systems Maturity Level monitoring under the UHC Act (RA 11223) â€” bureau-level data collection/validation calendars, indicator sets, and reporting cycles that its Calendar of Activities and performance targets will revolve around. The research covers what the bureau reports upward (DBM/COA/CSC) but not the program systems whose milestones dominate the bureau's actual activity calendar; confirm current tool versions with the bureau rather than encoding assumptions.
- Leave, DTR and attendance interactions with IPCR and the activity calendar: Missing: CSC Omnibus Rules on Leave (application via CS Form 6, leave credits), Daily Time Records (CS Form 48), flexible work arrangements (CSC MC 6 s. 2022), and overtime/CTO rules (CSC-DBM JC 1 and 2, s. 2015) â€” these determine the IPCR 90-day minimum rating period, feed the SPMS edge cases already planned, and put travel orders/leaves on the calendar of activities. Even if HR/leave is out of scope as a module, the data contract with whatever holds leave/attendance must be planned now.
- Authoritative working-day calendar for all deadline engines: Every planned deadline engine (SPMS dates, FOI working days, RA 11032 3/7/20-day SLAs, COA/DBM report deadlines with next-working-day rules) presupposes a Philippine working-day calendar, but no module owns it: annual holiday proclamations, ad-hoc suspensions of work (walang pasok), and local/Manila-specific holidays. Plan a core holiday/suspension calendar service as shared infrastructure, maintained per year.
- Cash advance and liquidation clocks for calendar activities (COA Circular 97-002): Trainings, meetings and field activities on the Calendar of Activities are commonly funded by special cash advances whose grant, use and liquidation deadlines are fixed by COA Circular 97-002 (and EO 77 for travel CAs); linking calendar events to cash-advance records and liquidation countdowns is absent from both the calendar and budget coverage.
- Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) compliance for the new data sets: CSM respondent demographics, FOI requester identities, IPCR ratings and adverse-action records, and custodian accountability ledgers are all personal data; the plan lacks NPC-facing requirements â€” privacy notices/consent text on the CSM instrument, DPO designation, registration of data processing systems where thresholds apply, breach-notification duty, and retention rules aligned with the NAP GRDS.
- Electronic signature validity for generated compliance forms (RA 8792 / PNPKI): The plan generates many signed regulator-facing PDFs (SPMS forms, BFARs, GAM appendices, QMS documents) but never addresses whether e-signatures are acceptable: RA 8792 recognizes electronic signatures, and DICT's PNPKI issues government digital certificates, yet COA/CSC/DBM acceptance varies by form. Decide per artifact whether the system output is the record or merely a print-for-wet-signature aid, and plan PNPKI or image-signature handling accordingly.
- Internal quality audit program (ISO 9001:2015 clause 9.2): Audit results are treated as a Management Review input, but no module owns the internal quality audit itself: annual audit programme, audit plans/checklists, trained internal auditor pool, audit reports and their NC linkage â€” mandatory for ISO certification/PBB validation and the natural upstream source of the qms_nonconformities register already planned.
- 8888 Citizens' Complaint Hotline and complaint-handling SLAs: Complaint intake is only covered as a CSM free-text field; EO 6, s. 2016 requires agencies to act on 8888 Citizens' Complaint Hotline referrals within 72 hours, and ARTA complaints/CCB (Contact Center ng Bayan) referrals need tracked case handling. The document tracking IS should model complaint referrals as a document type with the 72-hour clock.
- Agency annual/accomplishment report as a distinct artifact: The performance module produces accomplishment reports, but the consolidated agency Annual Report â€” a required Transparency Seal posting and the standard year-end accountability artifact assembled from BAR/FAR, OPCR results, CSM results and program accomplishments â€” is not planned as a generated output; it is the single strongest showcase for the unified reports/Government Outputs screen.
